## 🧭 How I work

```text
$ cat /etc/philosophy.conf
keep systems understandable
prefer open over opaque
automate the boring parts
break things, learn, rebuild better
own your data, or someone else will
```

I believe in open standards, technical independence and digital sovereignty. Too much of our data lives in a handful of hyperscaler clouds, and governments, research institutes and universities are starting to feel what that costs in privacy, lock-in and control. I'd rather build the alternative: **open source, self-hosted and fully under your own control.**

Right now I'm mostly working on:

- 🕸️ **Datacenter networking**: EVPN/VXLAN leaf-spine fabrics that don't fall over
- 🖥️ **Linux engineering**: from kernel to cluster, reproducible and low-maintenance
- 🧠 **Local AI**: NVIDIA DGX Spark, GPU computing and privacy-first AI on premises
- 🔐 **Post-quantum cryptography**: hybrid SSH key exchange, OpenPGP and age
- 🧘 **Living by** [the joy of missing out](slices_of_life/reminders/joy_of_missing_out/en.md)
