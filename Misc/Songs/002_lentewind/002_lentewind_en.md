# Lentewind — what the song is about

*Lentewind* (“Spring wind”) is a light, playful Dutch song about spring arriving.

- A bird in a tree makes up a strange story in the spring wind: a song without words that ends the way it began.
- The verses sketch little spring scenes: a cat on the windowsill that has had enough, a fish blowing bubbles, trees swaying in the sun, the carillon ringing over the rooftops, sparrows playing tag in the gutter and a tigress at Blijdorp zoo (Rotterdam) having a cub.
- With a wink it adds that Bach turned the tune into sonatas, Strauss into a waltz and a beat band counted the bars — while everyone outside sings out of tune — and that daddy has hay fever because it's spring.
- Everyone who hears it says happily: it's starting — in the spring wind.
