# Later als ik dood ben — what the song is about

*Later als ik dood ben* (“Later, when I'm dead”) is a Dutch song about what the singer hopes to be for the people he loves once he is gone.

- He imagines a lighter life after death: no longer busy with what is written down, but being a last straw to hold on to for people who are lost, a butterfly that makes everything lighter.
- He wants to live on in the eyes and the heart of the one he loves, to show her what he was blind to himself: that love is often simply there for the taking.
- He imagines pulling out all the stops of an organ, sound washing over the world with a love that takes the sting out of hatred.
- He no longer wants to think about the people he had to fight; he lost too much time on things that don't matter.
- He wants to become the father he never managed to be: a guide and a guardian angel on your shoulder, a father who is always there, even after he is gone.
- In the end he wants to dissolve into everything — the last blue of the evening sky — and dance with the one he loves without her knowing he is holding her.
