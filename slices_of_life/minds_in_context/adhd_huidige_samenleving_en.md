### A proposition about ADHD

People with ADHD are generally not regarded as “ill”, unlike, for example, people with psychotic disorders. You could argue that in a society that revolves less around deadlines, performance and productivity, people with ADHD might never have been given the ADHD label, or at least that their traits would not have been seen as a “disorder”.

From this perspective, ADHD could partly be seen as a by-product of the [neoliberal society](./neoliberale_samenleving_en.md) we live in today.
