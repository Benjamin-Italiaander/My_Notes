The expression **“beminnelijke deugd der inschikkelijkheid”** (roughly *“the lovable virtue of accommodation”*) sounds very old-fashioned and formal.
Here is what it means in normal, modern language:

### 📘 Meaning

* **Beminnelijke deugd** → an attractive, likeable quality
* **Inschikkelijkheid** → being accommodating, flexible, willing to adapt to others

### 👉 In short

**“De beminnelijke deugd der inschikkelijkheid”** means: *the lovely, likeable quality of someone who easily goes along with others and doesn't make things difficult.*

It describes someone who is friendly, flexible and pleasant to be around.

The expression **“aan een lub zitten konkelen”** is **old, colloquial language (dialect)** and is hardly used any more.
Here is what it means:

### 📘 Meaning

* **konkelen** → to fiddle, tinker, mess about secretively, do something sneaky or clumsy
* **lub** → a flap, edge or piece of fabric (for example of clothing), sometimes used more broadly for “some little thing you can fiddle with”

### 👉 In short

**“Aan een lub zitten konkelen”** means: *to sit fiddling or messing about with some little thing, often pointlessly or secretively.*

It can mean:

* nervously fiddling with your clothes
* secretly messing about with something
* clumsily fiddling with something
