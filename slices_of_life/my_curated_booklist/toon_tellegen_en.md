###### Toon Tellegen — *Wellustig mors ik om mij heen* (I spill around me with relish)

If you leave me now

I'll sit at a table, with a big white napkin tucked in,
old and flushed,
eating pies and enormous puddings —
**with relish I spill around me.**

If you now, behind me, on tiptoe, walk out the door

my mouth is full and I can't go on.
But footmen, my footmen, prise my jaws open
and whisper: just think of God, our god...

and slowly I slump down, slide downwards
and think of God,
on the big gleaming floor,
with in the doorway just the heel of your foot.

**If you leave me *now*...**
