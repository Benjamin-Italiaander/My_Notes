**Improving relationships (English)**

- *Love Phobia: How to Overcome Your Fear of Commitment and Abandonment* – Hannah Cuppen
- *Hold Me Tight: Seven Conversations for a Lifetime of Love* – [Sue Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sue_Johnson)

> *Hold Me Tight* is based on Emotionally Focused Therapy (there is also a separate Christian edition, *Created for Connection*). I strongly recommend it to all couples, regardless of their background, culture or religion.
> Its insights into emotional connection, healthy communication and secure bonding are universal and meaningful for anyone who wants to build a deeper, stronger and more loving relationship.
