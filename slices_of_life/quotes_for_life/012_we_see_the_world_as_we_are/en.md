# We see the world as we are

> **We don't see the world as it is; we see the world as we are. No more and no less.**

The world you perceive is your reality. What you see outside yourself is shaped in part by what lives inside you.

Your perception is like a mirror: your thoughts, feelings, expectations and beliefs colour the way you look at the world. What you see can therefore never be fully separated from the one who is looking.

Changing the world outside us is not always possible. But we *can* examine **how we look at that world**.

Change your inner perspective, and the world you experience changes with it.

**Perception is a consequence, not the cause.**
