# Loneliness disappears as soon as you restore the connection with yourself

**Deepak Chopra's view on loneliness**

Chopra argues that in the modern world we are constantly distracted by external stimuli. Because of this, we lose touch with our true essence.

- **The inner dialogue:** loneliness arises when your inner critic takes over.
- **Silence as medicine:** by seeking calm, you find your way back to who you really are.
