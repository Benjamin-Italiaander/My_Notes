# 🧩 Self-Care Checklist (Daily)

*Based on insights from* ***Running on Empty*** *by Jonice Webb*

## 🌿 Basic physical care

* [ ] Did I drink enough water today?
* [ ] Did I eat on time (not too little, not too much)?
* [ ] Did I choose food that is good for me?
* [ ] Did I take breaks when my body got tired?
* [ ] Did I move for at least 10 minutes today?

---

## 🧘‍♂️ Rest & recovery

* [ ] Did I have moments of real rest today (without a screen)?
* [ ] Did I consciously slow down my breathing for a moment?
* [ ] Did I do something today that lowers my stress?
* [ ] Did I say early enough that I was getting overstimulated?
* [ ] Did I end my evening calmly?

---

## 💛 Emotional check-in

* [ ] Did I take a moment today to notice what I feel?
* [ ] Did I allow that feeling without judgement?
* [ ] Did I take 1–2 minutes to simply let a feeling be there?
* [ ] Did I do something that supports my emotion (instead of suppressing it)?

---

## 🔄 Boundaries & balance

* [ ] Did I say “no” where it was needed?
* [ ] Did I skip something I actually didn't have the energy for?
* [ ] Did I avoid putting other people before myself at the expense of my own well-being?
* [ ] Did I ask for help when something felt too heavy?

---

## 🌼 Kindness towards myself

* [ ] Did I do one small kind thing for myself today?
* [ ] Did I avoid belittling myself or being harsh on myself?
* [ ] Did I treat myself the way I would treat a friend?
