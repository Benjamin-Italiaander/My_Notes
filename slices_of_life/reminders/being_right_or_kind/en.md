# Being right or being kind

The same reminder, in a few different words:

> If you have the choice between being right and being kind, most of the time it's better to choose kindness.

> When faced with the choice between proving you're right and being kind, kindness is usually the better choice.

> Being right may win an argument, but being kind often wins a person. When you can choose between the two, choose kindness.

> Not every battle needs to be won. If you can choose between being right and being kind, choose kindness.

> The need to be right is temporary; the impact of kindness can last a lifetime. Choose kindness whenever you can.
