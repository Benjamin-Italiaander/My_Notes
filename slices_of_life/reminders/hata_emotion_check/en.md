# **HATA – a quick emotion check**

*Based on* **Running on Empty** *by Jonice Webb*

---

## **1. H — Recognise** (*Herken*)

Pause for a moment and ask yourself:

* What *exactly* am I feeling right now?
* What do I notice in my body?
* What is the first emotion that comes up?

**Examples:**

* tension in your chest
* irritation
* empty / flat
* heart rate going up
* pressure in your head

---

## **2. A — Accept** (*Accepteer*)

Let the feeling be there without judgement.

Say something to yourself like:

* “This is what I feel right now.”
* “It's allowed to be here.”
* “I don't have to force myself.”

**No:**

* pushing it away
* judging yourself
* rationalising

---

## **3. T — Take time** (*Tijd geven*)

Take **90 seconds to 2 minutes** to simply let the feeling exist.

* breathe calmly
* stay in your body
* don't run away, don't distract yourself
* just *be* with the feeling

Note: emotions often behave like a wave — if you stay with it, it subsides by itself.

---

## **4. A — Act** (*Actie*)

Only now do you choose a **conscious** action.

**Examples of good actions:**

* a short walk or some fresh air
* a warm shower
* something to drink
* answering a message later, calmly
* briefly writing down what is going on
* setting a boundary
* taking a break

**Not-so-good actions:**

* reacting impulsively
* numbing yourself (food, scrolling, alcohol)
* escaping into work

---

## **Mini checklist**

* [ ] Did I feel what I feel?
* [ ] Did I allow it without judgement?
* [ ] Did I let it be there for 1–2 minutes?
* [ ] Am I now choosing an action that helps me (and doesn't harm me)?
