# Friendship, Loyalty, and Showing Up

I remember telling one of my close friends... We were riding bikes somewhere, and out of the blue (I don't know why it hit me) I turned to him and said: "You know, you're one of those friends that, if I was really in trouble, I would call."

His reaction: he didn't say thank you.
He said, **"I'd be mad if you didn't."**

The reason I bring it up is it's the same in a relationship, which is:

* when you don't call me and ask me for help,
* when you don't call me and say, "I need to cry,"
* when you don't call me and say, "I'm in the mud,"

you deny me the opportunity, the joy, the honour of sitting in the mud with you.
