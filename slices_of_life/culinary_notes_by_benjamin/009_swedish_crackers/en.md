# Swedish crackers

## Ingredients

- 6 dl rolled oats
- 1 dl oat bran (or another bran)
- 2 dl sunflower seeds
- 2 dl pumpkin seeds
- 2 dl flaxseed
- 1 tbsp dried herbs (e.g. herbes de Provence)
- 1 dl sunflower oil
- 7 dl water
- 1 tsp salt

## Method

1. Mix all the ingredients together well.
2. Let the mixture stand for at least 30 minutes.
   It can also stand for a few hours or even a whole day.
3. Spread the mixture out on a silicone baking mat,
   as thinly as possible (not on a wire rack).
4. Use the edge of a palette knife to lightly
   pre-mark the shapes.
5. Bake the crackers for 60–70 minutes at 160 °C (320 °F).
6. The crackers are done when they are completely dry
   and lightly coloured.

**Important:** The crackers must be completely dry.
This is the most important thing for a crispy result.

## Abbreviations

- dl = decilitre (100 ml, a little under ½ cup)
- tbsp = tablespoon
- tsp = teaspoon
