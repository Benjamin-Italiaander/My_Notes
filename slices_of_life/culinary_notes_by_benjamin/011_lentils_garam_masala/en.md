# 🍲 Lentils with Garam Masala

A simple, aromatic lentil dish with warm Indian spices. Delicious with rice.

---

## Ingredients

### Spice mix
- 2 tablespoons **garam masala**
- 1 tablespoon **cumin seeds**
- 1 tablespoon **turmeric**
- 1 teaspoon **chilli powder**
- 1 teaspoon **salt**

### Other
- 1 teaspoon **ghee** (or oil)
- 200–250 g **lentils**
- 100–200 g **tomato paste**
- Water (just enough to cover the lentils)
- **Rice** to serve

---

## Method

1. **Mix the spices**

   In a bowl, mix:
   - garam masala  
   - cumin seeds  
   - turmeric  
   - chilli powder  
   - salt  

2. **Toast the spices**

   Heat **1 teaspoon of ghee** in a pan over **low to medium heat**.  
   Add the spice mix and heat it for **30–60 seconds** to release the flavours.  
   Make sure the spices **don't burn**.

3. **Add the lentils**

   Add the **lentils** and pour in a little water so they are well covered.

4. **Cook**

   Let the lentils **simmer gently for about 20 minutes**. Don't let them boil dry; add water during cooking if needed, as the lentils should absorb the water.

5. **Add the tomato**

   After **15 minutes**, add the **tomato paste** and stir well.

6. **Serve**

   Serve the lentils **warm with rice**.

---

## Tip

For extra flavour, you can add at the end:

- fresh **coriander**
- a little **lemon juice**
- a spoonful of **yoghurt**
