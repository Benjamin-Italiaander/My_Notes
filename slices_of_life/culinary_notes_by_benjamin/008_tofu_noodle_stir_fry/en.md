# **Tofu Noodle Stir-Fry**

## **Ingredients**

### **Serves 4**

#### **Noodles & tofu**

* **225 g** linguine (or rice noodles)
* **400 g** extra-firm tofu (1 block), drained and pressed
* **2 tbsp cornstarch** (≈ **16 g**)
* **2 tbsp oil** (≈ **30 ml**, divided)

---

### **Sauce**

* **60 ml** soy sauce (low sodium)
* **15 ml** hoisin sauce
* **15 ml** sesame oil
* **15 ml** maple syrup
* **15 g** gochujang
* **15 ml** rice vinegar
* **1 tsp** sesame seeds (≈ **3 g**)
* **4 cloves** garlic, finely chopped
* **1 tsp** fresh ginger, grated (≈ **5 g**)

---

### **Vegetables**

* **2 bell peppers**, thinly sliced
* **1 yellow onion**, cut into rings
* **1 jalapeño**, thinly sliced (optional)
* **1 carrot**, grated (≈ **80–100 g**)

---

### **Toppings (optional)**

* Spring onion
* Sesame seeds
* Fresh basil

---

# **Method**

### **1. Cook the noodles**

Bring a large pot of water to the boil.
Add the **linguine** and cook until *al dente*.
Drain and rinse with cold water to prevent sticking.

---

### **2. Make the sauce**

In a bowl or jar, mix:

* 60 ml soy sauce
* 15 ml hoisin sauce
* 15 ml sesame oil
* 15 ml maple syrup
* 15 g gochujang
* 15 ml rice vinegar
* 3 g sesame seeds
* 4 cloves garlic
* 5 g ginger

Stir into a smooth sauce.

---

### **3. Prepare the tofu**

1. Cut the **400 g tofu** into cubes.
2. Put them in a bowl and sprinkle with the **16 g cornstarch**. Toss until everything is coated.
3. Heat **15 ml oil** in a large pan over medium heat.
4. Fry the tofu until golden brown on all sides.
5. Add **half** of the sauce and let it caramelise.
6. Remove the tofu from the pan and set aside.

---

### **4. Stir-fry the vegetables**

1. Heat the remaining **15 ml oil** in the same pan.
2. Add the bell pepper, onion, carrot and jalapeño (if using).
3. Stir-fry for 5 minutes until the vegetables start to soften and char slightly.

---

### **5. Bring it all together**

1. Add the cooked noodles to the pan with the vegetables.
2. Pour in the **rest of the sauce**.
3. Toss until everything is well coated.
4. Return the tofu and stir until everything is hot.

---

### **6. Serve**

Divide over bowls and garnish with:

* Spring onion
* Sesame seeds
* Fresh basil
